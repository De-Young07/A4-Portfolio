Public health systems

Lafiya

Offline-resilient disease surveillance

Core engineering project

Problem

Field surveillance and contact-tracing workflows can lose continuity when devices operate in places with intermittent connectivity. Data must be captured reliably and protected while it moves between devices and central systems.

Approach

I built the backend and data-engineering layer: schema-validated ingestion, privacy-preserving device identifiers, deterministic record IDs, deduplication, encrypted store-and-forward relay, and local SQLite persistence. Relay devices transport sealed bundles without reading the underlying health payloads. The resulting records feed downstream intelligence for exposure risk, vitals anomalies, contact networks, outbreak zones, and device health.

Evidence & stage

A substantial low-connectivity public-health engineering project developed with a three-person team. The initial implementation was built during HealthTrace Hackathon 2026, where the team placed first in System Design & Innovation. The recognition is one milestone; the work is presented here for its system design and public-health purpose.

Role: Backend and data engineeringPython · SQLite/PostgreSQL · REST APIs · encrypted relay · privacy-aware ingestion
Agriculture · optimisation

AmanKasu ↗

Agricultural trade intelligence (formerly TradeFlow NG)

Early field validation

Problem

Agricultural traders often make buy/sell and route decisions with fragmented price information. Surplus in one market may not reach a nearby market where demand and prices are stronger.

Approach

The platform combines weekly market-price collection by field agents, Prophet-based commodity price forecasts, and linear-programming optimisation. Recommendations consider buying and selling locations alongside transport cost, vehicle capacity, supply constraints, and risk. It is designed to support intra-state and inter-state market decisions as the data network grows.

Evidence & stage

The public project site reports 19 field agents across eight target states, physical field validation in three states, 12 trader interviews, and a 70-respondent demand survey spanning 16 states. These are validation and network-building indicators, not proof of national-scale impact.

Role: Chief Technology OfficerPython · Prophet · PuLP · PostgreSQL · Streamlit
Education · career readiness

NEXUS ↗

Student intelligence and career opportunity navigation

Live platform · initial testing

Problem

Tertiary students can reach graduation without a clear view of how their current skills map to industry-facing career pathways, what gaps remain, or which opportunities are relevant to them.

Approach

NEXUS maps more than 70 skills across 11 technology career pathways. Students build a profile, compare current skills against a selected direction, explore matching opportunities, and receive practical next steps and learning roadmaps. The intent is to connect self-assessment with opportunity discovery rather than function as a passive job board.

Evidence & stage

A working platform built for FUTMinna students. Initial user testing has explored whether students can identify skill gaps and discover relevant opportunities in one sitting. Broader validation of recommendation quality and student outcomes remains a next step.

Role: Concept and product directionNext.js · TypeScript · PostgreSQL · Supabase · Python
Retail operations · HealthTech

Keji Pharmacy ↗

Offline-first point-of-sale and management system

Live in store

Problem

Pharmacy operations depend on accurate stock, pricing, sales, and customer accounts. Cloud-only systems become unreliable when a store loses internet access, while owners still need remote visibility.

Approach

A local-first operational system runs on local PostgreSQL and synchronises to the cloud in the background. It includes four-tier pricing, FEFO batch tracking, inventory and sales workflows, a customer debt ledger, role-based access, Google Forms stock-entry integration, and a remote CEO dashboard.

Evidence & stage

The system is live in-store. The design keeps core shop operations available offline while providing remote oversight through cloud synchronisation. It was delivered with setup documentation, Windows deployment scripts, and versioned database migrations.

Mobility · marketplace systems

BetaRide ↗

Peer-to-peer urban mobility for Ilorin

Pilot corridor operations

Problem

Local commuters face fare uncertainty and fragmented access to commercial tricycles, motorcycles, and cars. Rigid surge pricing may not reflect local transport norms.

Approach

BetaRide supports benchmark-informed fare proposals, location-based dispatch to nearby operators, a single structured counter-offer, driver and vehicle checks, and direct passenger-to-driver settlement. The product is designed around locally defined corridors and transparent negotiation.

Evidence & stage

The public product site describes an active pilot corridor from Offa Garage Terminal to five strategic hubs in Ilorin and a 50-operator pilot fleet. These are pilot-stage indicators rather than evidence of citywide coverage.

Digital health · inclusion

WeNaijaHealth ↗

Multilingual health-insurance navigation for informal workers

Live public platform

Problem

Informal workers may struggle to understand health-insurance eligibility, costs, available schemes, and official registration routes. Language and process complexity can make access harder.

Approach

The platform provides a free, no-login, five-language navigation experience. Users share basic information about their work and location, explore relevant programme options such as KwaraCare and NHIA pathways, understand eligibility and cost considerations, and follow official registration steps. Team alerts and an operations triage console support navigation to verified clinic-enrolment pathways within a 24-hour response service window. It is an independent navigation platform, not an insurer or government agency.

Evidence & stage

The public website explains pathways to scheme information and support, including that coverage activation remains with the relevant scheme authority. My role is Lead Advocate for Health Insurance, supporting awareness and navigation. The 24-hour window is a service-response commitment, not a guarantee of insurance activation.

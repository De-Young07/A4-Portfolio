About Me

I build for the people
everyone else ignores.

"I thought I understood the problem — so I started building. I hit a dead end. That dead end became the real beginning."

I'm a final-year B.Tech Statistics student (GPA 4.30/5.00) at the Federal University of Technology Minna — one of Nigeria's leading STEM institutions — simultaneously completing a Data Science degree at WorldQuant University. My work sits at the intersection of statistical rigour and real-world deployment: not theory for its own sake, but science that ships.

I came into data science with a single question: why does so much perishable food move across Nigeria and rot before it reaches people who need it? I built what I thought was a solution. Then I hit the walls that matter — the market doesn't behave the way you modelled, traders don't trust an app built by someone who's never traded, and the data you assumed existed simply doesn't.

That failure became TradeFlow NG — a fully deployed agricultural trade optimization platform now active across 8 Nigerian states. Not rebuilt from scratch. Rebuilt from experience. That distinction is everything I know about engineering.

Today I build production systems across agriculture, health, civic technology, and finance. I serve as Director of Research at ASSN FUTMinna, Tech Lead at DSN FUTMinna, and Campus Ambassador for Young Innovators Nigeria. Every project begins with the same question: what does the person at the end of this system actually need? Not what's technically impressive. What solves the problem.

01
Agricultural Tech · FinTech
TradeFlow NG
Nigeria loses ~40% of perishable produce annually to supply chain inefficiency. Traders make buy-sell decisions blind. TradeFlow collects real-time commodity prices from field agents across 8 states, forecasts price trends using ARIMA, and runs a linear programming optimizer to tell traders exactly what to buy, where to source, and where to sell — before committing a single naira. Rebuilt after a full failure. Now fully deployed with admin dashboard, mobile agent interface, and cloud PostgreSQL database.

Philosophy
"The best technology starts with a deep understanding of the people it serves — not the tools available to build it."
01
Start with the problem. Not the model.
Too many data scientists fall in love with their methods before they understand what's actually broken. I spend more time defining the problem than choosing the algorithm — because a perfectly optimised solution to the wrong problem is still a failure.
02
Build systems, not experiments.
A 92% accuracy model that lives in a notebook helps nobody. I build pipelines that deploy, persist, monitor, and work in real environments — not ideal ones. If it can't handle production edge cases, it's not finished.
03
Dead ends are data.
TradeFlow NG was rebuilt after hitting every wall that matters. That restart — from experience, not from scratch — taught me more than any course. Every failure contains a specification for what to build next. Treat it that way.
04
The best tool is the one people actually use.
I build for the person at the end of the system — the trader, the health worker, the student, the government clerk. If they can't use it without a manual, I haven't finished building. Adoption is the final metric.
